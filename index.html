📋 Important Traffic Rules

  • Always wear a helmet while riding a two-wheeler.
  • Always wear a seat belt in a car.
  • Follow traffic signals and road signs.
  • Never use a mobile phone while driving.
  • Never drive under the influence of alcohol.
  • Follow speed limits.
  • Use zebra crossings to cross the road.

🛑 Road Safety Tips

🚗 Drive Slowly

Always maintain a safe speed.

🚶 Be Careful

Look both ways before crossing.

🚴 Stay Alert

Pay attention to the road.
